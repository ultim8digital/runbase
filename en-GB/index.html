FAQ

Good to know

Answers before you get started.

Does a short run count?

Yes. Even a small loop around a single map dot can count. The live preview shows the land you can claim before closing it.

Do I need an account?

There is no separate sign-up. Online play uses iCloud. Without iCloud you can practise offline against practice runners.

Can I buy land or advantages?

No. Land is claimed by running. Coins are earned in the game; there are no ads or in-app purchases.

Can other players see my route?

Your GPS route stays on your phone. Only claimed hexes are shared, and owners are anonymous on the public map.
